Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day delivery?

We offer next-day delivery for orders placed before 12pm, Monday to Friday. For urgent orders, call us directly.

Can I visit the roastery?

Yes! Our roastery café is open to the public. We also offer guided roastery tours every Saturday at 10am — booking required.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines. Regional orders ship within 3–5 business days.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Absolutely. We supply specialty cafés, hotels, and offices across Singapore. Email wholesale@emberoakcoffee.sg for a quote.
