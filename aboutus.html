How It All Began

A Spark in a Queenstown Garage

Ember & Oak Coffee Co. was born in 2009 when founders Lim Wei Jie and Sarah Tan converted a rented garage in Queenstown into Singapore's smallest specialty roastery. Armed with a second-hand drum roaster and a relentless curiosity, they spent two years experimenting with green beans sourced directly from farms they personally visited.

What began as weekend batches shared with neighbours quickly outgrew the garage. By 2012, the duo had established Singapore's first community-supported coffee model — connecting subscribers directly with the farms their coffee came from.

Today, Ember & Oak operates a 3,500 sq ft roastery in Queenstown, employs 32 passionate professionals, and ships freshly roasted coffee to customers across Southeast Asia. Yet we remain a family business at heart — still tasting every single batch before it leaves our facility.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Three pillars that guide everything we do — from sourcing decisions to your morning cup.

🎯

Our Mission

To connect coffee lovers with the world's most exceptional beans through transparent, ethical sourcing and the highest standards of artisan roasting — making every cup a meaningful experience.

🌟

Our Vision

A world where every coffee drinker knows the story behind their cup, and every farmer receives a fair and dignified return for their extraordinary labour. We exist to close that gap.

💎

Integrity — we never compromise on quality or ethics.
